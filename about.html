How they behave

Not a chatbot you babysit

It proposes, you approve, every time. You decide exactly what it can access and who's allowed to ask it to act.

Any workflow you can imagine, across your work tools, can be automated.

Built security-first from day one, so it's not just fast, it's safe to run your real work through. No bot contacts your customers. Every external send needs your OK for that exact message. Payments, banking and brokers stay human-only. Passwords never go in chat.

Specialists, not a pile of bots. Where jobs overlap, we fold them into one specialist per field, so you run a few strong bots instead of ten small ones.

The Foundry standard

How a bot gets on this site

  1. 16 checks before publishing. Hard rules present, no private data, a refusal test, a health-check routine, a category tag and a brand line.
  2. Versions step by 0.3. v0.3, v0.6, v1.0, v1.3 and on. Each release gets a changelog line.
  3. Three avatar options. One bold object, no people, no logos. You pick one at install.
  4. A companion bot. Related bots recommend each other both ways. Recommend only; never installed for you.
  5. Routines ship paused. You turn them on.
