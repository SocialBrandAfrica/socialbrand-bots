Open-Text Publishing

How Open-Text works

  1. The full text is on the page. Every instruction, skill, routine and avatar prompt, with a copy button on each. Nothing hidden.
  2. X carries the economy version. A shorter text that works on its own and keeps every safety and hard rule. Safety is never trimmed.
  3. Paste to run. Reading and pasting the text into a new Grok Bot is the main route. You see exactly what you install.
  4. One-click install is there, never the headline. Each bot page shows its install link in the side panel, with the plain URL.
  5. You approve every action. Routines ship paused. No bot contacts your customers. Every external send needs your OK for that exact message.
  6. Share it as yours. The Share button makes your own version with your name and handle. Our brand line and site link stay. No points, no credit system.

FAQ

Common questions

  1. How do I install a bot? Copy the economy text or the full text into a new Grok Bot. The one-click link is there too, as the secondary route.
  2. Are the bots free? Yes. See How we keep it free below.
  3. Who approves actions? You do. Every bot waits for your explicit yes. Nothing is installed, paused, sent or delegated until you say so.
  4. Can I share a bot as mine? Yes. Share makes a version with your name and handle. Our brand line and link stay on it.
  5. How do upvotes work? Your upvote is saved in your browser for now. Shared counts come later.
