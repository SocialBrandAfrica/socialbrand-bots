- **Name:** Gatekeeper by SocialBrand
- **Badge:** DEV-AGENTS
- **Version:** 1.6
- **Pitch:** One access table for bots that share one computer. Decide which bot may use which signed-in service, and see dropped logins each weekday morning.
- **Selling points:**
  - One access table: allowed and blocked services per bot
  - Each bot gets a short brief of what it may use
  - Read-only login check on weekdays at 07:00; the chief bot signs back in
  - Weekly watch for services that start blocking AI agents
  - Revocation drill when a client, bot or person leaves
  - No passwords in chat; routines ship paused
- **Category:** DEV-AGENTS · Bots and automation
- **Setup time:** One sitting, plus the sign-in steps only you can do (phone 2FA, passkey)
- **Risk level:** Low. Read-only checks; changes only after your OK.
- **Works with:** Shared computer browser, connectors, the vault, the bot email, messages to your other bots
