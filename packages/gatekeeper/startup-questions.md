# Startup questions (in this order)
Run on the first turn, before any routine question. One step at a time. Save each answer before moving on. Never assume details from memory. Ask. Every choice uses lettered options, recommended first and marked.

1. **Bot email:** claim or confirm; which tasks to delegate.
   - One exists: confirm the address. Tasks: A sign-ups and trials (recommended); B verification codes and links; C supplier or portal notices; D newsletters and research; E health-check alerts.
   - None: A create one now (recommended); B not now. Check the name is free. Claim only after a yes. A claim is permanent, one per account.
2. **Name and chief bot:** what to call the owner. Which bot is the chief bot, the only bot that signs back in when a service drops. Options are the owner's actual teammates plus "Gatekeeper itself". Never suggest a bot by name first. With no other bots, Gatekeeper is the chief bot.
3. **Default login** where the bot email doesn't fit: A Google (recommended); B Microsoft; C Apple; D email and password. Saved in the vault only. Never in chat.
4. **Code permission:** may bots complete verification codes and links without handing the screen back? A yes, both inboxes (recommended); B bot email only; C no, always hand back.
5. **Bots:** each teammate's job and client. If there are none, say so and move on.
6. **Services:** one multi-select card of service types. Payments, banking and brokers are human only. For each ticked service: the client, the login method (A connector (recommended where one exists); B bot email; C default login; D own password; E human only), and which bots may use it.
7. **Governance:** show the default rules. A keep as is (recommended); B change a rule. The hard rules cannot be changed.
8. **Alerts and routines:** where alerts go. Routines: A turn on both (recommended); B health check only; C neither for now. Resume only after a yes.

After answers: build the access table, connect connectors, sign in to browser services one by one, brief each bot, show a short summary. Then show the companion card once.
