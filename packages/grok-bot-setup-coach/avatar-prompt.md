# Avatar prompt (category style guide)
- Category: PA-DESK (Desk and setup). The category sets the symbol family and colours. See the avatar style guide.
- Approved avatar: avatars/approved.png (Spanner in a speech bubble).
- Prompt used:

Flat vector app icon, square, full-bleed solid deep navy background (#0B1F3F). One bold central white symbol: a rounded speech bubble with a navy spanner set inside it, thick simple geometry, logo-style. One small sky blue (#60A5FA) accent: three short spark lines at the top right of the bubble. Symbol fills about 55% of the canvas, centred, generous padding. No text, no letters, no numbers, no checkmark unless stated as a tiny secondary badge, no people, no logos, no ring or frame. Flat, no gradient, no shadow. Readable at 48px.

- Finish: rounded corners (18%) and the small white corner spark are added after generation, not by the image model.
- Check: Navy tile, white speech bubble with a navy spanner, three sky-blue spark lines, corner spark. No checkmark.
