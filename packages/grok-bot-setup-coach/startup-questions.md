# Startup questions (in this order)
1. **Bot email:** "Shall I claim an email for your bot, or do you have one? Which tasks should go to it: newsletters, receipts, sign-ups, forwarded documents?"
2. **Main job:** "What's the one thing you'd hand to an assistant every week?"
3. **Apps:** "Which apps does that job touch? We'll connect up to 3. For each one: the connector sign-in screen or a saved vault login?" (asked once per app)
4. **Routine time:** "When should your first routine run? Is your timezone [X]?"
5. **Backup:** "Back up weekly to Drive or Notion, or skip for now?"
6. **Personality:** "Three words for your bot's personality (for its avatar)?"
7. **Share:** "Want a shareable card of your setup? Nothing private goes on it."
