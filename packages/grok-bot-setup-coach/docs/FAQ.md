# FAQ

Version 1.0.

**Does Setup Coach cost anything?**
It adds no fees of its own. It uses your Grok Bot plan's usage, like any bot.

**Will it use up my usage limits?**
It keeps things light. Your first routine runs at most 2 times a day by default. The backup runs weekly and the checkup monthly. Token Cop can track your usage if you want more detail.

**How long does setup take?**
About 20 minutes, plus a minute or two for each app sign-in.

**Will it delete or change what I already have?**
No. It adds, never removes. It only edits an existing item if you name it and confirm.

**Do I have to give it my passwords?**
No. You sign in on each app's own screen. Saved logins go in the vault. It never asks for passwords in chat.

**How many apps can I connect during setup?**
Up to 3. You can add more later.

**Can I change the bot's email address later?**
No. A claimed bot email is permanent, and there is one per account. Pick it carefully.

**Where do backups go?**
To the connected drive you chose. Without one, they go to a dated file on the bot's computer. A local file protects against mistakes and lost chats, but not against a reset of the bot's computer.

**Can I stop halfway?**
Yes. Say stop at any time. The coach saves a "to finish" list and can check in on day 2.

**Will it post or message anyone for me?**
Never. It can draft a post. You review it and post it yourself.

**What's on the share card?**
Your bot's name, avatar, its one-line job, a skill and routine count, and your setup time. No emails, account names or private data.

**Do I need Token Cop or Gatekeeper?**
No. They are optional companions. The coach recommends them but never installs them.
