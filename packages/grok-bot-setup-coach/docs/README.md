# Grok Bot Setup Coach

By SocialBrand · socialbrand.africa · https://bots.socialbrand.africa

## What it does
Setup Coach gets a new Grok Bot working in one sitting. It walks you through:
- a name for your bot
- the bot's own email address
- up to 3 connected apps
- a first skill for your main job
- a first routine that runs on its own
- a weekly backup and a monthly checkup
- an avatar
- a shareable "my bot" card

It shows every change before making it. Nothing is saved until you say yes.

## Who it's for
New Grok Bot owners who want a working setup today. You don't need any tech skills.

## Startup questions
The coach asks one question at a time, as lettered options A to E:
1. What should your bot be called?
2. Which email address should the bot use, and what goes to it?
3. What's the one thing you'd hand to an assistant every week?
4. Which apps does that job need (up to 3)?
5. Which first routine do you want, and at what time?
6. Which shipped routines should be switched on: both, only the backup, or neither?
7. Keep the starting avatar, or make a new one?
8. Which share card format: square, 4:5 portrait, or both?

## Routines
Both routines ship paused. The coach asks at setup whether to switch them on.

| Routine | When it runs (your timezone) | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly backup | Sundays around 20:00 | Saves your memory summary, skill list, routine list and connector list. It only adds new copies. It messages you only if it fails. |
| Monthly setup checkup | The 1st of each month around 09:00 | A read-only health check. It sends one short report. Fixes need your yes. |

Both may start up to 15 minutes early or late.

**To pause one:** ask the coach, for example "pause the weekly backup". You can also find it in the bot's Routines tab. To switch it back on, ask "resume the weekly backup".

Your first routine runs at most 2 times a day by default.

## Skills

## Companion bots
- **Token Cop** (https://bots.socialbrand.africa/bots/token-cop): tracks your Grok usage so limits don't catch you by surprise. The coach shows its card once at the end of setup.
- **Gatekeeper** (https://x.ai/bot/D03y99U4zXKh0cshnhgah): governs which bot may use which logged-in site. The coach mentions it only if you run several bots.

The coach recommends these. It never installs them.

## Tone
Friendly. The first hello is upbeat. After that it uses short, calm, factual sentences. Longer messages use bullets and a few emoji. Warnings, approvals and errors never use emoji.

## What it never does without your yes
- Save a skill, routine, memory or connector.
- Rename the bot, claim an email or change the avatar (apart from the starting avatar).
- Edit or delete anything you already have.
- Send or post anything.

## What it never does at all
- Contact anyone for you.
- Handle payments, banking, brokers, medical or legal actions.
- Ask for passwords, tokens, card or ID numbers in chat.
- Put your email, account names or private data on the share card.
