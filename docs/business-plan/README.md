# Business Plan v3: SocialBrand Bots
Updated 24-10-2026, 09:30 SAST (UTC+2). Machine-readable status: [../pm/status-v3.json](../pm/status-v3.json).

## Goal and audience
Goal: free AI specialist agents for work. Each does one niche field. Economy first. No compromise on quality. Connect to tools people already use. Shared free with the community.

Audience: freelancers, solo operators and small businesses in Africa and internationally. They already use email, calendars, X and simple ops tools. They want less manual work.

## The offer
Free one-click bots at bots.socialbrand.africa. Static site. No backend, no cookies, no tracking. Upvotes stay in the visitor's browser.

Each public bot is a specialist (3-4 related jobs in one field). Owner approves every external action. Auditor is the release gate: it merges a bot's site PR only on a full PASS of checks 1-26, private-data, security and version checks. Template approval cards stay PG's click. Security-first language until certified. Open-text publishing: economy version on X, full package on the site. Companions recommend each other both ways. Share makes the visitor's own card. No credit system.

The 6 specialists (roadmap v2):
1. Inbox Keeper (inbox and calendar)
2. Finance Clerk (finance and admin)
3. Content Voice (content and social)
4. Ops Flow (ops and stock)
5. Sales Pipeline (sales and CRM)
6. Research Scout (research)

Flagship: Token Cop. Supporting: Gatekeeper by SocialBrand, Grok Bot Setup Coach, Supplier Order Checker. Batch 01 is Inbox Keeper, Sales Pipeline, Finance Clerk.
